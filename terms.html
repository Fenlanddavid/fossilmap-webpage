About FossilMap

FossilMap is a free field recording platform for UK fossil hunters and amateur palaeontologists. It includes specimen recording, locality management, field trip tracking, tide planning and geological reference tools.

FossilMap is provided free of charge. No account is required. Your specimens, localities and photos are stored locally on your own device and never transmitted to our servers.
